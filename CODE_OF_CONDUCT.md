# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the
work. Harassment or personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential issue or contacting a
maintainer. Reports will be reviewed and acted on.
